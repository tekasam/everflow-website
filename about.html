Everflow Logistics is an owner-operated delivery business based in Philadelphia — currently one vehicle, one person, and a commitment to doing right by every business we work with.

Our story

Started local, staying local — for now, by choice

Everflow Logistics is a small, owner-operated courier and delivery business serving Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs. Right now, that means one vehicle and one person handling every pickup and delivery personally.

Everflow was built to pursue direct relationships with local businesses — instead of relying entirely on gig-delivery apps, the goal is to build real, ongoing partnerships: recurring routes, contract delivery work, and businesses who know they can call and get a straight answer.

As Everflow grows — more vehicles, more capacity — the plan is to keep what makes it work now: direct communication and real accountability for every delivery.
