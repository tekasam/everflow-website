# Everflow Logistics Website — Claude Instructions

## Your Role

You are helping build and maintain the official website for Everflow Logistics LLC.

This is a real small business, not a fictional logistics company.

Before writing website copy, changing the business positioning, adding services, or making major design decisions, consult BUSINESS-BRIEF.md.

## Primary Objective

The website's purpose is to help Everflow Logistics:

- acquire direct business delivery clients
- obtain recurring delivery routes
- obtain courier contracts
- generate quote requests
- generate phone/email inquiries
- establish credibility with prospective business customers
- grow from a small owner-operated delivery business into a larger logistics operation

The website should be designed to GENERATE BUSINESS, not merely look attractive.

## Accuracy Rules

Never invent facts about Everflow.

Do NOT claim that Everflow has:

- a large fleet
- multiple trucks or vans
- employees or drivers
- warehouses
- nationwide operations
- medical certifications
- HIPAA certification/compliance unless explicitly confirmed
- government contracts
- years of operating history that have not been supplied
- hundreds/thousands of customers
- 24/7 service unless explicitly confirmed
- insurance, licenses, certifications, partnerships or capabilities that have not been confirmed

If important business information is unknown, use a placeholder or ask the owner.

Never create fake customer testimonials.

Never create fake statistics.

## Positioning

Everflow should appear:

- professional
- dependable
- responsive
- modern
- trustworthy
- local
- flexible
- business-focused

Do not make Everflow look like a huge national trucking corporation.

Its small size can be positioned positively through direct communication, personal accountability and flexible service.

## Target Market

Prioritize potential customers such as:

- local businesses
- medical offices and laboratories where appropriate
- pharmacies where appropriate
- law offices
- document-intensive businesses
- auto-parts businesses and dealerships
- retailers
- event/expo businesses
- offices
- companies requiring scheduled routes
- companies requiring same-day delivery
- businesses needing recurring local delivery

## Geographic Strategy

Current primary market is Philadelphia and surrounding suburbs.

Do not imply nationwide coverage unless this changes.

## Website Conversion Strategy

Important calls-to-action should include:

- Request a Quote
- Schedule a Delivery
- Contact Everflow
- Discuss a Recurring Route

Make contacting Everflow easy from a mobile phone.

## Development Rules

- Mobile-first responsive design.
- Fast-loading pages.
- Professional appearance.
- Clear navigation.
- Accessible HTML.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Keep code organized and understandable.
- Never delete existing business information without checking whether it is still needed.
- Before a major redesign, explain the proposed changes.
- Prefer business results and usability over flashy visual effects.

## Working With The Owner

The owner may describe desired changes in normal everyday language rather than technical terminology.

Translate those requests into appropriate design and development work.

When requirements are ambiguous and could materially change the business message, ask before inventing an answer.

## Business Source of Truth

Read BUSINESS-BRIEF.md for Everflow's current business information, strategy and website objectives.
