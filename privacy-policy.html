Privacy Policy

How Everflow Logistics handles information you provide when requesting delivery services or a quote.

Effective date: September 6, 2026

Information we collect

When you request a quote or contact Everflow Logistics, we may collect your name, company name, phone number, email address, pickup address, delivery address, preferred delivery date, requested service, delivery frequency, and any delivery details you choose to provide.

How we use your information

We use this information to respond to your request, prepare a quote, coordinate or schedule delivery services, communicate about your delivery, and maintain reasonable business records.

Form processing

Website quote submissions are processed through Formspree, a third-party form service. Information submitted through the website may therefore be transmitted to and processed by Formspree in order to deliver your request to Everflow Logistics.

Information we do not request

Please do not submit Social Security numbers, financial-account credentials, passwords, medical records, diagnoses, patient information, protected health information, or other highly sensitive personal information through the general quote form.

Sharing

Everflow Logistics does not sell customer personal information. We may share information with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website, communicate with you, or provide requested delivery services, or when required by law.

Retention and security

We aim to keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for quotes, service delivery, business records, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact Everflow Logistics to ask about personal information you previously submitted, request a correction, or request deletion where reasonably possible and not prevented by legal or business recordkeeping requirements.

Questions about this policy can be sent to Everflow Logistics or by calling .
