Medical Courier Services

Delivery support for medical and healthcare offices

Transport of appropriate medical office documents, general supplies, and equipment for medical practices, dental offices, and similar businesses in the Philadelphia area.

  • Documents, general supplies, and equipment
  • Same-day, rush, or recurring scheduling available
  • Direct, careful handling every time

What this doesn't include: Everflow does not currently hold specimen-transport certification, pharmaceutical licensing, or HIPAA-specific certification, and does not present itself as qualified for regulated specimen or controlled-substance transport. If your practice has a specific requirement, contact us directly and we'll talk through it honestly.
